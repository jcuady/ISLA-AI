Two controls that replace
a policy nobody follows

Staff will paste a customer record into an AI tool. The only control that holds is one that runs where the data already is — and verifies its own work.

Egress Guard

Detects 18 classes of Philippine banking PII — SSS in its three real layouts, TIN, GCash numbers that are just mobiles with no marker, card PANs validated by Luhn, remittance references — then redacts with stable pseudonyms. It re-runs the entire detector over its own output before releasing a single character.

fail-closed on residual → BLOCK

DPA Copilot

Taglish in, cited statute out. Hybrid dense + BM25 retrieval over 223 citable chunks. It refuses when the evidence is thin rather than inventing a circular number.

ASK

Audit ledger

Hash-chained records of every action — SHA-256, each entry linked to the last. Stores counts and verdicts, never customer text.

Real air-gap, not a badge that says so

The status chip reports an actual socket probe against public resolvers. Pull the cable mid-demo and the answer changes in front of the judges. Nothing in this page can reach the network either — the Content-Security-Policy is default-src 'none', so a future edit that adds a CDN dependency fails loudly instead of quietly leaking.

Refuses when it must

Correct refusal is a feature, not a fallback. Ask it something outside the corpus and it answers in Taglish that it has no basis, and cites nothing — measured at 100% on out-of-domain questions. That is the behaviour that stops a model inventing a circular number. In this industry a confident wrong answer is worse than no answer.
