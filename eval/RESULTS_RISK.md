> **How precision is measured, and why it is conservative.** Each scenario in the dataset is labelled with its *dominant* typology, but a real mule scenario also describes structuring and a cross-border transfer, and the engine correctly reports all three. Those extra findings are counted as false positives here, which understates true precision. That is deliberate: labelling every typology a scenario genuinely describes would mean writing the expected output after seeing it, which is the thing this evaluation exists to prevent. The number below is therefore a floor, not a best estimate.
